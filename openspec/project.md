# Project Context: Seinagro Corporate Landing Page

## Brand Identity & Business Overview

- **Company Name:** Seinagro (Standardized spelling; never use "Sinagro" or old repository brand names)
- **Headquarters:** Egypt
- **Target Markets:** Egypt, African Continent, Arab Region, Asian & International B2B partners
- **Core Business Pillars:**
  1. Production and development of certified, exclusive, high-yielding strategic crop seeds (climate-change resilient).
  2. Trading and distribution of straight, compound, and specialized organic fertilizers.
- **Brand Positioning:** Newly established company backed by founders with decades of executive leadership in multinational agricultural corporations across Egypt, Africa, and Asia. Scientific excellence combined with hands-on field experience.
- **Visual Assets Ready:**
  - Logo: located in `public/assets/`
  - Hero Image: located in `public/assets/`

## Vision & Mission

- **Vision:** To be among the pioneers in the seed and fertilizer production industry in Egypt and the region through investing in agricultural creativity and innovation.
- **Mission:** To leverage the local and international expertise of Seinagro’s founders in the agricultural sector to deliver high-quality products.

## Strategic Objectives (Core Content Pillars)

1. **Climate-Resilient R&D:** Develop and produce exclusive high-yielding seed varieties, plus straight and organic fertilizers designed to build resilience against climate change.
2. **Uncompromising Quality:** Uphold strict quality standards across all products and operational processes to earn the enduring trust of farmers and B2B partners.
3. **Regional & Global Expansion:** Strengthen market share in Egypt while executing targeted expansion across African, Arab, and global markets.
4. **Scientific & Field Human Capital:** Invest in an elite workforce blending top scientific qualifications with practical field experience.
5. **Global Tech Transfer:** Leverage founders' multinational experience to adopt the latest global technologies in seed production and marketing.

## Technical & Architectural Constraints

- **Supported Locales:** Arabic (`ar` - RTL), English (`en` - LTR), French (`fr` - LTR).
- **Strict RTL/LTR Rule:** Use CSS logical properties (`ms-*`, `me-*`, `ps-*`, `pe-*`, `start-*`, `end-*`, `text-start`, `text-end`) exclusively.
- **Translation Parity:** Every text string must be simultaneously written in `ar` (Modern Standard Business Arabic), `en`, and `fr` (International/African B2B French) dictionary files. No hardcoded strings in components.
